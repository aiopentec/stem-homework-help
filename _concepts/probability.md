# Probability

Probability assigns a number between 0 and 1 to an event, representing how likely that event is to occur, where 0 means impossible and 1 means certain.

## What is probability?

Probability assigns a number between 0 and 1 to an event, representing how likely that event is to occur, where 0 means impossible and 1 means certain.

## Basic formula

For equally likely outcomes, P(event) = (number of favorable outcomes) / (total number of outcomes). More generally, probabilities must satisfy P(Ω) = 1 and be non-negative and additive over disjoint events.

## Example

Rolling a fair six-sided die, the probability of rolling an even number is 3/6 = 1/2, since 3 of the 6 equally likely outcomes (2, 4, 6) are favorable.

## Common mistakes

Confusing P(A and B) with P(A or B); treating dependent events as independent; forgetting that probabilities of an exhaustive set of outcomes must sum to 1.
