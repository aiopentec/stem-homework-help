## 1.  What the problem is asking (in plain language)

*We are given an infinite field \(K\).  
We say that **polynomials are almost surjective over \(K\)** if every non‑constant polynomial  

\[
f(x)\in K[x]
\]

has the property that the set of values it misses in \(K\) is **finite**; i.e. for all but finitely many \(a\in K\) the equation  

\[
f(x)=a
\]

has a solution \(x\in K\).
